Privacy policy.

Larkin turns your conversations into notes and insights. That means it handles some of the most personal things you say. This page explains what we collect, where it goes, how long we keep it, and what you control.

Last updated 7 October 2026. Applies to the Larkin beta app for iPhone and Apple Watch, and to getlarkin.com.

The short version

  • Larkin records only when you start it on your Watch, and you can stop it any time.
  • Audio is used to make a transcript, then deleted. We keep the words, not the recordings.
  • Your transcripts, notes and insights are private to your account. We don’t sell them, advertise with them, or use them to train AI models.
  • We use a few service providers to run Larkin: Supabase (storage), Deepgram (transcription), OpenAI (notes and insights) and PostHog (usage counts, never conversation content).
  • You can delete your account in the app. That removes your account, transcripts, notes and stored audio from our servers.
  • The people around you may be recorded too. Tell them, and stop when they ask.

Who we are

Larkin is operated by Julien Nérée, its founder (“Larkin”, “we”, “us”), ahead of forming a company. When a company takes over, we’ll update this page and tell you before anything changes. For anything about this policy or your data, write to support@getlarkin.com.

Larkin is in beta. The app, and this policy, will change as we learn. We’ll tell you about important changes before they apply.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and an email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). You also tell the app the name you’d like Larkin to use.

Your conversations

  • Audio. While you’re recording, your Apple Watch captures audio in one-minute pieces. Pieces with no speech may be skipped on the Watch. The rest are sent to our servers, through your iPhone or directly from the Watch, to be transcribed.
  • Transcripts. The text of what was said, with times. Transcripts stay in your account.
  • What Larkin writes from them. Conversation notes, day summaries, to-dos, insights about how you communicate, your communication report and focus, and suggested facts about you.
  • What you add. Facts you confirm or correct, questions you ask Larkin, and feedback on insights.

Usage and technical information

  • Usage counts. Which screens and features are used, onboarding steps, and counts and durations such as minutes captured or conversations in a day. These go to PostHog, linked to your account identifier. They never include audio, transcripts, notes, titles, quotes, chat messages or names.
  • App and device details needed to deliver recordings, such as app version, delivery status and error states.
  • Diagnostics you choose to send. Settings › Diagnostics can export a report about how your recordings reached Larkin. It’s only sent if you share it.

When you contact us

If you write to us by email, WhatsApp or Telegram, we receive your message and contact details. WhatsApp and Telegram handle those messages under their own privacy policies.

On getlarkin.com

Our website counts visits with PostHog, without cookies: which pages are viewed, the site that sent you, and your rough location and device type, never your name or anything you type. It also uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits from our ads. If you reserve a Larkin, payment is handled by Stripe; we receive your order details and email, not your card number.

How we use it

  • To turn your recordings into transcripts, notes, summaries and insights, and to show them to you.
  • To answer your questions in “Ask Larkin”, using the conversations you ask about.
  • To publish notes you choose to share, and stop them when you ask.
  • To keep Larkin working: delivering recordings, fixing problems, preventing abuse and keeping accounts secure.
  • To understand, from usage counts, which parts of Larkin are useful, so we can improve it.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don’t sell your data. We don’t use your conversations for advertising. We don’t use your conversations to train AI models, and our providers don’t either (see below).

The people around you

Larkin’s microphone hears everyone nearby, so other people’s words can end up in your transcripts and notes. Larkin doesn’t identify other people, doesn’t build profiles of them, and doesn’t send voice prints off your phone. Its insights are about you.

You’re responsible for using Larkin lawfully: tell people you’re recording, respect requests to stop, and follow the recording rules where you are. If someone asks you to remove a conversation they were part of, you can delete it in the app or contact us for help.

Sharing notes

You can share a conversation’s notes as a link. You choose the sections and see a preview first. Anyone with the link can read them until you tap Stop sharing. Shared pages never include audio, transcripts, quotes or chats.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop recording any time from the Watch.
  • Review facts Larkin suggests about you, and confirm, correct or reject them.
  • Stop sharing any shared note.
  • Delete your account in Settings › Account. This deletes your account, transcripts, shared notes and stored audio from our servers straight away, and clears Larkin’s data from your iPhone the next time you open the app. Audio still waiting on your Watch is no longer uploaded.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete your usage counts, or to stop a particular use, by writing to support@getlarkin.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or Singapore’s PDPA, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

We’ll answer within 30 days.

Security

Data travels encrypted. Each account can only reach its own data, enforced by the database itself. Recordings are uploaded with short-lived, account-specific permissions. Our providers encrypt stored data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we’ll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

Larkin is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using Larkin, contact us and we’ll delete their account.

Changes

When we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For important changes, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
