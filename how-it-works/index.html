Start on your wrist.

Short audio pieces wait on the Watch until delivery is confirmed. They can travel directly to the cloud or through your phone. If there’s no connection, they wait for one. Recording also hears people around you: tell them, get permission where needed, and respect their choice.

What happens to the recording

The words stay.
The audio goes.

Once your phone imports a completed transcript, it deletes the cloud recording and then its local audio copy. The text remains in your account.

  1. Record
  2. Transcribe
  3. Import text
  4. Delete audio

A note on timing. Audio can remain while an upload or phone import is waiting, or if cleanup fails. The beta currently has no fixed deletion deadline in those cases.

Your own space

Your transcripts aren’t publicly browseable. Account checks keep other users from opening your recordings and transcripts.

Protected on the way.

Cloud transfers use HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit.

Shared only when you choose.

Select and preview note sections before publishing a link. Raw audio, transcripts and chat aren’t included. Anyone with an active link can read it; Stop sharing revokes access.

Cloud processing, explained.

Larkin’s service systems and cloud providers can access content to process it. This is not end-to-end encryption that excludes Larkin and its providers.

A few useful details

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Current beta behavior, reviewed on 7 October 2026.

Is audio deleted immediately?

No. The Watch waits for confirmed delivery before removing its copy. The phone removes cloud audio after it imports completed text, then removes its local source audio. Offline periods and cleanup failures can delay that process. Independent server cleanup with a fixed deadline is still pending.

Who can access my transcripts?

Other app users cannot browse your account’s transcripts. Larkin’s backend services and processing providers handle content to run the app. Selected shared notes are readable by anyone who has their active link. Raw transcripts are not included in those shared pages.

Is my content used to train AI?

Larkin’s Deepgram transcription requests explicitly opt out of model improvement. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default, unless the customer opts in. Larkin sends requests with response storage disabled. That setting is separate from provider safety logs, which can retain content under the provider’s policy.

What if I’m offline?

Undelivered audio waits locally for a connection. Notes and insights arrive after cloud processing and phone import. Audio deletion may also wait for the phone to complete its part.

What stays on my phone, and what’s in the cloud?

The cloud holds transcripts in your account and temporary audio for processing. Your phone keeps the journal you read, your insights, personal facts and chats, and saves a backup copy of them to your account. If you delete the app or change phones, signing in brings back your transcripts and that backup.

Does removing a conversation erase its transcript?

In the current beta, removing a conversation hides that note from your journal. It does not erase the underlying cloud transcript or revoke an existing share link. Use Stop sharing to revoke a link. Account deletion removes Larkin’s account records and stored audio; it does not change providers’ separate retention rules.

Does Larkin record other people too?

Yes, the microphone can hear the people around you. Tell them before recording, respect requests to stop, and follow the rules that apply where you are. The app does not identify every speaker or determine everyone’s consent for you.
