What happens to your voice in the current Watch and iPhone beta.

Audio is temporary.

After the phone imports completed text, it deletes the cloud recording and its local audio copy. Waiting imports and cleanup failures can delay deletion.

You control recording.

Use Stop on the Watch to stop new capture. Previously recorded pieces may still finish processing.

Protected in transit.

Cloud transfers use HTTPS to encrypt data on the way. Account checks keep other users from reading your transcripts.

Questions

Good to know.

Which Apple Watch and iPhone do I need?

An Apple Watch on watchOS 11 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 18 or later.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. When the app becomes a paid membership, everyone who reserved a Founder Edition bracelet keeps it free, for life.

Does it record everything I say?

The current Watch beta records while it is running. Audio is removed after delivery and phone import, with delays possible while offline or if cleanup fails. See what happens to the audio.

How do I stop it listening?

In the current beta, use Stop on the Watch. This stops new capture; pieces already recorded may still finish processing.

Do I need my iPhone with me?

Not while recording. The watch keeps what it recorded until it can deliver it. Your notes and insights then appear on your iPhone.

What about recording laws where I live?

Tell the people around you before recording, respect their requests to stop, and follow the rules where you are. The app does not determine everyone’s consent or the law for you.

What happens when the bracelet ships?

Your account, your days and your insights carry over. The bracelet takes over the listening, and the app stays the same. Reserve one for $10.
