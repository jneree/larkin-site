Privacy

Private and secure by design.

What happens to your voice in the current Watch and iPhone beta.

Audio is temporary.

After the phone imports completed text, it deletes the cloud recording and its local audio copy. Waiting imports and cleanup failures can delay deletion.

You control recording.

Use Stop on the Watch to stop new capture. Previously recorded pieces may still finish processing.

Protected in transit.

Cloud transfers use HTTPS to encrypt data on the way. Account checks keep other users from reading your transcripts.

Clear about deletion.

Removing a note hides it from your journal. Account deletion removes Larkin’s stored account data and audio. Provider log retention is separate.

Cloud services, explained.

Supabase hosts your account, Deepgram transcribes audio, and OpenAI processes text for notes and insights.

Private by default.

Your transcripts aren’t publicly browseable. Sharing selected notes is your choice, and you can revoke their link.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it record everything I say?

The current Watch beta records while it is running. Audio is removed after delivery and phone import, with delays possible while offline or if cleanup fails. See what happens to the audio.

How do I stop it listening?

In the current beta, use Stop on the Watch. This stops new capture; pieces already recorded may still finish processing.

What about recording laws where I live?

Tell the people around you before recording, respect their requests to stop, and follow the rules where you are. The app does not determine everyone’s consent or the law for you.

Do I need my phone with me?

No. The band holds about three days on its own and syncs when your phone is nearby.

How long does the battery last?

Seven days of normal wear, around 160 hours of listening. Ninety minutes on the magnetic charger takes it back to full.

Can I try Larkin before the bracelet ships?

Yes. The Larkin app is in beta on Apple Watch and iPhone, free for everyone. Get your invite and your watch does the listening until your bracelet arrives.

What does the lifetime membership include?

The app is free for everyone during the beta. When it becomes a paid membership, everyone who reserved a Founder Edition keeps it free, for life. If you cancel your reservation and take back your $10, the membership goes with it.

What does it cost, and when does it ship?
