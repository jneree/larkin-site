Questions

Does it record everything I say?+

It listens while you're wearing it, but it doesn't keep the audio. Speech is turned into text and the recording is deleted — the only sound kept is a highlight you pressed to save.

How do I stop it listening?+

Hold the module for two seconds. It stops until you hold it again. You can also pause it from the app.

What about recording laws where I live?+

They differ by country and by state. The app tells you what applies where you are.

Do I need my phone with me?+

No. The band holds about three days on its own and syncs when your phone is nearby.

How long does the battery last?+

Seven days of normal wear, around 160 hours of listening. Ninety minutes on the magnetic charger takes it back to full.
